HJ 633-2026, table 3
====================

Transcription of table 3 (page 3 of the standard) of :download:`HJ 633-2026
<hj633-2026.pdf>`, "空气质量分指数及对应的污染物项目浓度限值" (IAQI and the
corresponding pollutant concentration limits). The PDF is a scan without a
text layer, this table was read from the page image and is what
``aqi.algos.mep.MEP_2026`` is built from.

Units: µg/m³, except CO in mg/m³. "-" means the standard gives no value,
see the notes.

Notes (from the standard):

a. The 1 hour averages of SO2, NO2, CO and O3 are only used for real time
   reports.
b. For SO2 1 hour averages above 800 µg/m³, the IAQI is 200.
c. For O3 8 hour averages above 800 µg/m³, the IAQI is 300.

Changes from HJ 633-2012
------------------------

Compared with the ``MEP_2012`` table, only two cells change, both at IAQI 100:

* PM10 24 hour: 150 → **120** µg/m³
* PM2.5 24 hour: 75 → **60** µg/m³

Everything else (IAQI 0 to 500 for the other pollutants and for PM10 and PM2.5
above 100) is identical.

Cross-check
-----------

The limits of IAQI 100 are the secondary (class two) concentration limits of
the ambient air quality standard. Table 1 of :download:`GB 3095-2026
<gb3095-2026.pdf>` (page 3, transition stage, secondary limits) reads the
same: SO2 24 hour 150 and 1 hour 500, NO2 80 and 200, CO 4 and 10, O3 8 hour
160 and 1 hour 200, **PM10 24 hour 120** and **PM2.5 24 hour 60** µg/m³ (CO in
mg/m³). This confirms the two changed cells. The other cells equal the
HJ 633-2012 table, which was read from a text layer.

Calculation clauses
-------------------

The clauses of the standard that ``MEP_2026`` applies (translated):

* 4.2.2: concentrations in µg/m³ are rounded to integers following GB/T 8170,
  CO in mg/m³ to one decimal.
* 4.2.4: the IAQI is computed with formula (1), interpolating between the
  neighbouring limits of table 3, and the AQI is the highest IAQI (formula 2).
* 4.2.6: IAQI and AQI results are all rounded up to an integer ("全部向上进位取整数"),
  with no decimals. HJ 633-2012 clause 6.2 said the same in fewer words
  ("全部进位取整数").
